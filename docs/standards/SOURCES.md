## Reading scope

Ten PDFs were selected. Relevant clauses and full pages were read for the
subjects listed below; this was not a cover-to-cover conformity assessment.
The ISO 26262-6 coverage table (PDF p. 33), IEC 61508-2 memory diagnostic
table (PDF p. 56) and MISRA title page (PDF p. 3) were also rendered and
visually checked. Extracted text was used for navigation; table interpretation
retains the surrounding qualifications.

The IEC 61508:2010 series and ISO 26262:2018 series are the editions of the
local sources. These notes do not assert that every local document is the
latest publication. Select the applicable edition explicitly for any real
assessment; a newer draft or amendment is not silently incorporated here.
